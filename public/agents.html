Each bot is a character: what it does, its traits (what it can't do, and what it does instead), and what it still needs. Ask any of them for a new ability: the Box sorts the ask within 15 minutes into an instruction change (Mimi approves it), a build (filed to the Box), or a can't (added to its traits, with what it can do instead). You can also edit what each bot does directly. Every save is a new version, the Box uses it on the bot's next run, and any earlier version can be put back. What no edit can change: bots only write drafts into Review, never publish, post, send or spend, and every draft is checked for em dashes and AI tells.
